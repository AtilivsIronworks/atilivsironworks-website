Selected work

Anglo-Saxon Broken Back Seax

Coppergate, York · Multi-bar pattern weld

About this piece

Wrought iron spine, two alternating twisted bars of 1075 and 15N20, and a wrought iron and 1080 edge bar joined with a wolf-tooth weld. Sheath design follows leatherwork recovered in the Coppergate excavations.
