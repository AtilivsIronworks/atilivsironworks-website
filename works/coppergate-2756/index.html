Coppergate Knife 2756

York · Catalogued reconstruction

The Project

Reconstruction of catalogue number 2756 from the 16-22 Coppergate site. Blade 153mm, tang 150mm, spine 6mm, angle-back A1 profile. Wrought iron spine butt-welded to a 1084 edge, 60 HRC. Walnut handle with a 1mm brass pommel plate.

← All works
At a glance
Inspiration

Knife 2756 from 16–22 Coppergate, York; angle-back A1 reconstruction.

Blade Dimensions

Blade length 153 mm; spine thickness 6 mm. Tang length 150 mm is recorded in the construction notes, but finished handle and overall lengths are not.

Work Materials

Blade: wrought-iron spine butt-welded to a 1084 steel edge. Handle: walnut. Non-ferrous fitting: 1 mm brass pommel plate.
