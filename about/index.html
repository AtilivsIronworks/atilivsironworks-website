About Atilivs Ironworks

Historical bladesmithing, informed by the surviving record.

I'm Corey Peterson. I forge historical blades by hand in Vernal, Utah, under the name Atilivs Ironworks.

First and foremost, I'm a husband, a father, and a historical reenactor. Bladesmithing is a side gig, something I do alongside my regular job and family life. It matters to me, but it isn't the only thing I do.

The work centers on pattern-welded seaxes and Anglo-Saxon knives, with Roman, Norse, and medieval pieces alongside. I started with rebar in a charcoal forge fed by a blow dryer. The tools got better. The standard stayed the same: study the find, name the sources, and be honest about what's documented and what's a modern choice.

In the Society for Creative Anachronism I'm known as Atilivs Primvs Castvs. Reenactment is a major part of my life, and it keeps me interested in the history behind the objects I make. The blades I build serve in tournaments, ceremonies, and on the belts of people who use them.

If you want a piece with real history behind it, look through the works and get in touch. I also share what I'm learning in the research and articles sections.
